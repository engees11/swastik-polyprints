Who We Are

Weaving Quality
Since 2002

M/s. Swastik Poly Prints Pvt. Ltd. is a premier textile process house situated at Plot No. 285-286, Road No. 2, G.I.D.C. Sachin, Surat. Incorporated under the Companies Act 1956, we have been delivering world-class fabric processing for over two decades.

Our state-of-the-art facility specialises in dyeing & printing of M.M.F. fabrics — Polyester × Polyester, Polyester × Viscose, and other challenging synthetic blends — all meeting AZO Free and PC Free standards.

What We Do

Our Core Services

End-to-end textile processing under one roof — from greige fabric to finished goods.

Fabric Dyeing

Advanced continuous & batch dyeing processes for polyester and blended fabrics with consistent colour fastness.

Fabric Printing

High-resolution rotary & flat-screen printing delivering vibrant, long-lasting patterns on all fabric types.

MMF Processing

Specialised processing of Man-Made Fibres including Polyester × Polyester & Polyester × Viscose blends.

AZO Free Processing

Certified AZO-free & PC-free processing ensuring compliance with international safety & environmental standards.

Quality Assurance

Rigorous in-process & final QA checks ensuring colour consistency, dimensional stability, and defect-free output.

Bulk Production

Scalable production capacity to handle high-volume orders with consistent quality & on-time delivery.
